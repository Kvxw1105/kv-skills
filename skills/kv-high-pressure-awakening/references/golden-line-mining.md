## 1. What counts as an explosive mother line

A golden line is not merely elegant, quotable, or emotionally loud. An **explosive mother line** must do at least three jobs at once:

- create immediate tension or contradiction;
- implicate the listener personally;
- leave an unresolved causal gap that demands explanation;
- contain enough conceptual pressure to support 40-90 seconds of expansion;
- sound complete as a sentence while remaining open as a question.

A useful test:

> If the line is spoken alone, will the listener feel both “this concerns me” and “explain why”?

Weak quotation:

> 自律很重要。

Explosive mother line:

> 你每天等动力，其实是在把人生的启动权交给心情。

The second line contains a target, a reversal, a mechanism, and an expansion path.

## 2. Why one line can support one video

A strong mother line is a compressed argument. Unpack it through four layers:

1. **Mirror**: what filmable behavior proves the line?
2. **Mechanism**: why does the behavior keep repeating?
3. **Future**: what will the pattern harden into?
4. **Choice**: what single action interrupts it?

Do not pad a line with synonyms. Each expansion layer must add a new reward.

## 3. Hook functions

Classify every candidate by its primary psychological function.

### A. Explanation seizure

Overturn the audience's preferred diagnosis.

- 你缺的可能早已不是时间，是你不肯为重要的事清场。
- 动力没有背叛你，你从一开始就不该把命交给它。

### B. Self-deception exposure

Reveal the conflict between identity claims and evidence.

- 你最擅长的努力，是做完所有不会暴露水平的事。
- 你一直在准备改变，准备本身却成了你逃避改变的地方。

### C. Future-loss projection

Make time and habit visible before the loss becomes irreversible.

- 你以为自己停在原地，其实低标准每天都在把你往后拖。
- 最可怕的衰老，是选择还在，心气先没了。

### D. Pain reversal

Challenge the worship or misuse of suffering.

- 吃苦不会自动让人变强，很多人只是在错误里反复受伤。
- 没留下判断的痛苦，只是披着勋章的损耗。

### E. Identity pressure

Turn one current action into evidence of who is being trained.

- 你今天完成的不是一项任务，是在训练谁替你过完这一生。
- 每一次向困难退让，都在教身体以后怎样逃跑。

### F. Hope rebound

Restore agency after pressure without giving free comfort.

- 你已经浪费过时间，但门还没有关；代价是今天必须留下证据。
- 旧习惯已经很强，可它仍然怕一件事：你开始连续完成。

### G. Causal or philosophical reversal

Best for Xuanqi and higher-level content.

- 门外人怕失败，门内人先看见自己为何舍不得接受检验。
- 人最深的沉睡，发生在他已经忘记自己睡着的时候。

### H. Image strike

Use a coherent image to make an abstract mechanism visible.

- 时间不会敲门，它只会安静地搬走你的选择。
- 你把每一次明天再说，都砌成了未来牢房的一块砖。

## 4. Mining workflow

When reading a long source, do not highlight only the loudest sentences. Mine each psychological section in six passes.

### Pass 1: Locate section claims

Mark every sentence that could summarize a section's new psychological claim. Ignore repeated intensifiers.

### Pass 2: Identify function

Label each claim as seizure, exposure, future loss, pain reversal, identity, hope, causal reversal, or image strike.

### Pass 3: Extract the mechanism, not the wording

Write the underlying relation in plain form:

```text
claimed desire + contradictory behavior + hidden protection + future cost
```

### Pass 4: Rebuild an original mother line

Change the syntax, image, emphasis, and causal framing. Preserve no distinctive sentence sequence from the source.

### Pass 5: Test expansion capacity

A candidate must support at least three of these routes:

- a modern micro-scene;
- a psychological explanation;
- a future scene;
- an immediate action;
- a role-specific interpretation;
- a counterexample or boundary.

### Pass 6: Deduplicate by psychological conflict

Two lines with different vocabulary but the same conflict are one atom. Keep the sharper line and move the other into supporting copy.

## 5. Scoring model

Score each candidate from 0-2 on six dimensions. Keep lines scoring 8 or above.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal implication | distant | partially relevant | directly addresses listener |
| Reversal | common statement | mild contrast | overturns a preferred explanation |
| Open loop | fully resolved | some curiosity | demands causal explanation |
| Spoken rhythm | awkward | usable | clean, forceful, memorable |
| Expansion capacity | one point only | one route | three or more routes |
| Originality | cliché | familiar with twist | fresh naming or image |

A line with high aggression but low expansion capacity is a slogan, not a mother line.

## 6. Expansion routes

For each retained line, choose one primary route. Do not force every line through the same seven-stage order.

### Mirror route

```text
mother line -> two concrete behaviors -> contradiction -> named mechanism -> one action
```

### Future route

```text
mother line -> time jump -> lost option -> emotional recognition -> remaining door
```

### Mechanism route

```text
mother line -> common explanation -> hidden causal chain -> boundary -> decision rule
```

### Story route

```text
mother line -> ordinary scene -> failed attempt -> reversal -> consequence -> choice
```

### Xuanqi threshold route

```text
mother line -> 门外人的理解 -> 门内人的因果 -> 一道门缝 -> 凡人的选择
```

### Grayce execution route

```text
mother line -> evidence audit -> cost calculation -> deadline -> visible output
```

## 7. Source-derived original hook bank

These lines are rebuilt from the supplied motivational source's mechanisms. They are not verbatim extractions. Each can open an independent short video.

### Motivation and discipline

1. 你越依赖动力，越容易在真正困难的时候失去行动能力。
2. 情绪只能点火，秩序才能带你走过没有火的日子。
3. 你不缺下一句狠话，你缺一次听完以后留下的现实证据。
4. 自律最强的时刻，是你终于不再每天询问自己想不想做。
5. 热血让你感觉正在改变，完成才会让现实承认你改变过。

### False effort and avoidance

6. 你最擅长的努力，可能恰好避开了所有会检验你的事情。
7. 很多人没有停止努力，他们只是一直在做不会失败的准备。
8. 忙碌最大的骗局，是让你筋疲力尽以后仍然没有结果。
9. 你嘴上说想赢，时间却一直投给了不会让你赢的东西。
10. 计划做得越漂亮，有时越能掩盖第一步迟迟没有发生。

### Fear and potential protection

11. 你舍不得开始，可能因为潜力一旦接受检验，就再也不能无限想象。
12. 有些人怕的并非失败，而是成功以后再也没有借口可用。
13. 只要永远不交卷，你就能永远相信自己本来会得高分。
14. 拖延保护的往往不是时间，是那个尚未被现实击碎的自我形象。
15. 你一直说还没准备好，其实是在等待一个不会暴露自己的机会。

### Time and regret

16. 时间最狠的地方，是它离开时几乎没有声音。
17. 你以为今天只是少做了一点，习惯却在替未来的你降低标准。
18. 人很少亲手放弃梦想，他通常只是把开始一再推到明天。
19. 最可怕的未来，是机会还在，你却已经不相信自己配得上它。
20. 你并没有停在原地，重复的退让正在把你训练成另一个人。
21. 六十岁的你未必恨今天不够成功，他可能更恨你从未真正出手。

### Pain, strength, and strategy

22. 痛苦不负责塑造你，它只负责暴露你有没有能力从中带走东西。
23. 没有反馈的坚持，可能只是把同一个错误练得越来越熟。
24. 真正危险的努力，是代价越来越大，方向却从未接受审判。
25. 伤疤只能证明你受过伤，无法证明你走对了路。
26. 强大并不等于永远加码，有时它表现为敢于停止无意义的损耗。

### Responsibility and rescue

27. 别人可以给你地图，却无法替你承担走错路的责任。
28. 没人能替你开完这一生，但你仍然可以向懂路的人借一把钥匙。
29. 等人来救最隐蔽的代价，是你慢慢忘了自己也能作出选择。
30. 帮助可以外借，人生最后那一步无法外包。

### Identity and creation

31. 每天真正被建造的，除了结果，还有那个将来替你作决定的人。
32. 消费别人的力量很容易，创造一份自己的证据才开始改变身份。
33. 你今天退后的那一步，会成为身体明天最熟悉的路线。
34. 自律成为身份以后，行动才不再是一场每日公投。
35. 一个人的未来，很大一部分藏在他无人看见时反复做的事里。

### Xuanqi inside/outside

36. 门外人寻找更狠的声音，门内人追问自己为何总在听懂以后沉睡。
37. 门外人把痛苦当作入场券，门内人只看它有没有留下判断。
38. 门外人害怕门关上，门内人更怕自己明明看见门，却一次次转身。
39. 门外人等待命运发出信号，门内人知道重复本身就在书写命运。
40. 玄启能让你看见门缝，无法替你在现实里留下脚印。

## 8. Role adaptation

Do not merely replace nouns.

- **Grayce**: sharpen cost, deadline, evidence, and tactical action. Prefer lines 6-10, 17, 23-24, 31-35.
- **Xinwu**: ground the line in relapse, shame, ordinary scenes, and one paid choice. Prefer lines 3, 13-15, 18-21, 32-33.
- **Xuanqi**: reduce command density and expand causal reversal through door, sleep, light, time, and choice. Prefer lines 16, 18-20, 27-30, 36-40.
- **Neutral**: preserve broad recognizability and use one concrete modern scene.

## 9. Failure modes

- Extracting only profanity or high-volume lines.
- Treating every sentence as a hook and flooding the bank with duplicates.
- Writing a complete conclusion that leaves no open loop.
- Using an abstract line that cannot produce filmable evidence.
- Making every hook follow “你不是……而是……”.
- Mistaking a title, verdict, or ending line for an opening mother line.
- Copying distinctive source wording instead of rebuilding the mechanism.
- Expanding a hook through repetition rather than new layers.

## 10. Delivery format

When the user asks to extract golden lines from a source, use this default table or equivalent compact structure:

| Mother line | Function | Expansion direction | Best voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| ... | explanation seizure | mirror + mechanism | Grayce |

Then select the strongest lines and demonstrate how one mother line becomes one complete video. Do not write every expansion unless requested; prioritize genuinely different atoms.
