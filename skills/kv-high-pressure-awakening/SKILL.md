# High-Pressure Awakening Engine

## Core mission

Transform motivational material into controlled emotional engineering. Preserve pressure, rhythm, imagery, confrontation, and action impulse while adding real insight, concrete scenes, ethical pressure boundaries, and distinct character voices.

Do not imitate a source line by line. Extract its attention mechanics, emotional sequence, sentence rhythm, imagery system, and identity logic, then rebuild original copy around a new topic.

## Task routing

1. **Diagnose a viral source**: read `references/diagnosis-and-remix.md`, identify the source's loops, rewards, images, identity choices, repetition, and transferable mechanisms.
2. **Mine golden-line hooks**: read `references/golden-line-mining.md`. Extract explosive mother lines by psychological function, rebuild them in original wording, score expansion capacity, and map each line to a distinct video direction.
3. **Create an atomic short video**: use the seven-stage loop below and read `references/core-engine.md` when stronger control is needed. A mother line may supply the opening, but the body must add mirror, mechanism, future, and choice rather than repeat the hook.
4. **Split a long speech into many videos**: separate by psychological conflict, not timestamps. Each atom must have one enemy, one fear, one image system, one action, and one identity choice. Use the mother-line map to reopen attention.
5. **Create a long speech**: sequence multiple atoms from surface behavior to hidden fear, time cost, identity, and immediate action. Insert a fresh mother line every 30-60 seconds.
6. **Adapt a character voice**: read `references/role-adapters.md`. Keep the engine separate from the voice layer.
7. **Produce reusable prompts or workflows**: read `references/prompt-recipes.md`.
8. **Check quality against examples**: read `references/gold-examples.md` and optionally run `scripts/check_copy.py` on a saved draft.

## Seven-stage awakening loop

Build one complete loop in this order:

1. **Seize the explanation**: overturn the audience's preferred excuse or diagnosis.
2. **Hold up the mirror**: show two to four filmable actions that contradict what they claim to want.
3. **Name the self-deception**: compress the contradiction into a memorable mechanism or condition.
4. **Build the future prison**: show a concrete continuation of the current habit. Use time, lost options, eroded self-trust, or identity hardening.
5. **Open a remaining door**: restore agency at the emotional low point. Hope must carry a cost.
6. **Give one weapon**: demand one immediate, observable, verifiable action.
7. **Rename the person**: frame the action as training a new identity, then stop on a repeatable closing line.

The loop must contain pressure, recognition, hope, action, and identity change. Missing pressure produces ordinary encouragement. Missing hope produces emotional abuse. Missing action produces stimulation without change.

## Atomic-video constraints

For a 40-90 second script:

- Use one behavioral contradiction.
- Use one hidden fear.
- Use one named enemy or mechanism.
- Use one primary image system: war, forging, door, mirror, time, cage, hunt, tide, or another coherent system.
- Use one future loss.
- Use one immediate action.
- Use one identity shift.
- Give a new reward every 12-20 seconds: a scene, naming, reversal, image, action, or judgment.

Do not stretch one conclusion by paraphrasing it repeatedly.

## Long-form architecture

Arrange atoms in an escalation:

```text
excuse -> observable behavior -> hidden fear -> time cost -> meaning of pain -> identity -> choice -> immediate action
```

Each atom must work independently. Reopen attention with a new claim, question, image, or future scene before the previous emotional energy decays.

A long script may repeat the thesis, but each return must add a new psychological layer. Do not merely increase volume or profanity.

## Source transformation workflow

Before writing, extract:

- 5-20 candidate mother lines, their psychological function, and expansion route;
- audience's stated desire;
- audience's repeated behavior;
- the contradiction between speech and evidence;
- hidden fear protected by inaction;
- enemy or mechanism to name;
- concrete future loss;
- believable remaining chance;
- one immediate action;
- desired identity;
- dominant image system;
- target voice and pressure level.

Then rebuild from these elements. Preserve no distinctive sequence of source sentences and no unsupported autobiographical claims. When a candidate line is strong only because of its original wording, extract the causal mechanism and rebuild a new line before using it.

## Voice separation

Treat the engine as structure and the character as a second layer.

- **Grayce**: high pressure, short commands, deadlines, resources, evidence, strategy, and consequences.
- **Xinwu**: lived struggle, relapse, shame, self-recognition, rough honesty, and a real choice made by an ordinary person.
- **Xuanqi**: low command density, high causal vision, door/inside/outside/sleep/light imagery, compassionate severity, and sparse revelation. Xuanqi changes the question; a mortal completes the action.
- **Neutral high pressure**: direct second-person voice, concrete scenes, controlled aggression, broad audience compatibility.

Never let every character sound like the same motivational speaker.

## Pressure control

Choose one level:

- **Level 1 — Cold awakening**: restrained, exact, low insult, high recognition.
- **Level 2 — Hard confrontation**: direct accusation, sharper contrast, firm action demand.
- **Level 3 — Battlefield intensity**: strong rhythm, forceful imagery, limited profanity, maximum urgency.

Do not use Level 3 by default. Match platform, audience relationship, topic, and character.

## Value upgrade

A strong script should add at least two of the following beyond emotional stimulation:

- a new psychological naming;
- a filmable modern scene;
- a correct non-consensus judgment;
- a verifiable action;
- a boundary or condition;
- a transferable decision rule;
- a clearer causal explanation.

Do not confuse cruelty with insight. The copy should make the audience see more clearly, not merely feel smaller.

## Hard boundaries

- Attack excuses, behaviors, avoidance loops, and self-deception. Do not attack illness, disability, poverty, trauma, or immutable identity.
- Do not dismiss legitimate mental-health conditions as weakness.
- Do not glorify sleep deprivation, injury, starvation, unsafe training, reckless financial risk, or permanent overwork.
- Do not promise universal success from effort alone.
- Do not invent first-person poverty, wealth, fame, business, or recovery experiences.
- Do not reduce every life path to money, entrepreneurship, masculinity, status, or domination.
- Do not mechanically use binary winner/loser identities. Bind identity pressure to the current choice and behavior.
- Avoid repeated use of formulaic constructions such as “你不是……而是……”, “真正的……”, and empty “现在就行动”.

## Delivery defaults

When the user requests a finished script, provide the finished script directly. Do not expose the whole framework unless they request analysis, a prompt, or a system.

When mining or splitting a source, first give the mother-line map: line, hook function, expansion direction, and best voice. Then give the content map and strongest finished atoms. Do not create near-duplicate outputs only to increase quantity.

When the user requests a full video asset set, separate title, cover phrase, voiceover script, description, and pinned comment. Do not mix metadata into spoken copy.

## Quality gate

Before delivery, confirm:

- The first sentence works as an explosive mother line: personal, tension-bearing, causally open, and expandable.
- The hook can support at least three new layers instead of only slogan repetition.
- The audience can recognize at least one concrete behavior.
- The named mechanism explains the contradiction.
- The future cost is specific and plausible.
- Hope appears before pressure becomes exhausting.
- The action is singular, immediate, and verifiable.
- The ending changes identity without declaring the audience inherently inferior.
- The imagery remains coherent.
- Each 12-20 seconds yields something new.
- The selected character voice is unmistakable.
