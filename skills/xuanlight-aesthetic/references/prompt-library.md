# Xuanlight Prompt Library

## Abstract master prompt CN

```text
一张具有玄光美学气质的视觉作品：幽暗、空寂、辽阔的世界中，一束神性微光照见孤独主体。整体风格融合东方留白、低清颗粒、柔和光晕、诗性氛围与克制的神秘感。画面强调空灵、宿命、寂静与高级感，不依赖复杂元素堆砌，而依赖光、留白、孤独主体与微妙情绪建立感染力。
```

## Abstract master prompt EN

```text
A visual work in the Xuanlight Aesthetic: within a dark, spacious, and quiet world, a sacred subtle light reveals a solitary subject. The overall style blends oriental negative space, low-resolution grain, soft halation, poetic atmosphere, and restrained mystery. The image should feel lonely, sublime, still, and high-end, relying not on clutter but on light, emptiness, and emotional concentration.
```

## Generic master prompt CN

```text
以玄光美学方式创作一张图：主体置于空旷幽暗的环境中，被一种神性天光、月轮、星轮或灵核般的光源照见。画面具有留白、低清颗粒、轻微失焦、柔和 bloom、胶片噪点与清冷色调。整体气质应空灵、克制、孤独、诗性，可以融合东方水墨、仙侠意境、数据灵魂感、废墟感或梦境叙事，但必须保持高级与简洁。
```

## Generic master prompt EN

```text
Create an image in the Xuanlight Aesthetic: place the subject in a dark, spacious, quiet environment, illuminated by a sacred celestial light, moonlike disc, star wheel, or glowing soul-core. The image should feature negative space, low-resolution grain, subtle blur, soft bloom, film noise, and a cool restrained palette. The mood should be lonely, poetic, restrained, and sublime, while optionally blending oriental ink-wash, xianxia atmosphere, soulcore sci-fi, ruins, or dreamlike interior storytelling.
```

## Xuanlight Ink EN

```text
An image in Xuanlight Ink: within a spacious oriental landscape, distant mountains, mist, a cold pond, isolated peaks, and negative space form the visual order. The subject may be a lone figure, a traveler, a pavilion, an object, or even an empty scene, illuminated by a restrained sacred skylight or moonlike disc. The image should feature xuan-paper texture, ink diffusion, low-resolution grain, subtle blur, and a cool palette of moon-white and mist-blue.
```

## Xuanlight Pilgrimage CN

```text

```

## Xuanlight Pilgrimage EN

```text
An image in Xuanlight Pilgrimage: a solitary cultivator, swordsman, wanderer, monk, or celestial traveler stands among clouds, mountains, shallow water, snow, or a cliffside, illuminated by a sacred skylight, moon-disc, or star wheel above. Emphasize silhouette, posture, and destiny rather than flashy effects, using minimal composition, cool tones, low-resolution grain, and soft halation.
```

## Xuanlight Soulcore CN


## Xuanlight Soulcore EN

```text
An image in Xuanlight Soulcore: a solitary subject appears as a consciousness-form, soul-body, or translucent being reconstructed from data and memory, with a faint sacred glowing core in the chest or center. The environment is dark and spacious, optionally including a distant city, reflective water, void-like space, a room, or a wasteland. Emphasize spiritual sci-fi, poetic loneliness, and low-resolution grain, avoiding excessive tech UI.
```

## Xuanlight Ruins EN

The figure, object, or empty scene stands in a vast quiet environment, revealed by distant light, skylight, or a sacred source. ## Xuanlight Room EN

```text
An image in Xuanlight Room: a quiet, worn interior is illuminated by only a small amount of light, which may come from a window, a television, a doorway, moonlight, or a glowing subject. The room may contain a person, a ghostly figure, a memory trace, an old object, or a solitary still object. ## Xuanlight Panels CN
```

```text
以玄光美学做成漫画或分镜风格：保持统一的暗基底、神性光源、低清颗粒与清冷色调，用镜头切换推进情绪与叙事。每一格只承载一个动作或一个情绪重点，利用留白、远近景变化与光线关系建立节奏。整体要像电影静帧般克制而有诗意。
```

## Xuanlight Panels EN

```text
Apply the Xuanlight Aesthetic to comic or storyboard form: maintain a unified dark base, sacred light source, low-resolution grain, and cool palette, and use shot changes to advance mood and narrative. Each panel should carry only one action or emotional focus, using negative space, shifts in distance, and light relationships to build rhythm. The result should feel restrained and poetic, like cinematic still frames.
```
