# Xuanlight Workflows

## Standard prompt workflow

1. Identify output type: image, cover, KV, character, scene, series, manga, or style document.
2. Select branch or blend: Ink, Pilgrimage, Soulcore, Ruins, Room, Panels.
3. Define the sacred light source.
4. Define the subject and the emptiness around it.
5. Choose density: minimal, balanced, narrative.
6. Output abstract, generic, and concrete prompts.
7. Add negative prompt, parameters, aspect ratio advice, and iteration directions.

## Scale workflow for multiple images

Freeze these before generating variants:

- branch or branch blend
- palette
- sacred light type
- grain and optical texture
- density level
- subject identity or symbol system
- composition family

Vary these across images:

- camera distance
- subject action
- setting details
- time of day
- emotional beat
- object symbols

## Cover and KV workflow

Use xuanlight restraint while leaving room for text:

- Keep one strong visual axis: light above, subject below, or light off-frame revealing the subject.
- Reserve a calm text zone with negative space.
- Avoid clutter behind expected title areas.
- Make the subject readable at thumbnail size.
- Use sacred light as brand memory, not just decoration.

## Manga workflow

Panel grammar:

- wide shot: establish world scale and light source.
- medium shot: show subject relationship to space.
- close shot: show emotional symbol, hand, object, chest core, reflection, or light on fabric.
- silent panel: use emptiness and light as emotional punctuation.

Rules:

- one emotional beat per panel.
- keep character anchors stable.
- keep palette, grain, and light logic consistent.
- avoid filling every panel with spectacle.

## Aspect ratio guidance

### 1:1
Use iconic central composition. Best for covers, avatars, and single emotional images.

### 4:5 / 9:16
Use vertical destiny axis. Sacred light above, subject below. Best for posters and mobile covers.

### 16:9 / 21:9
Use landscape scale, horizon, city, mountain range, water, or cinematic emptiness. Best for KV and worldbuilding.

## Iteration workflow

After a generation, diagnose with these questions:

1. Is it too busy? Reduce ornaments and objects.
2. Is it too generic? Strengthen sacred light and subject-symbol logic.
3. Is it too cyberpunk? Remove UI and neon clutter.
4. Is it too plain? Add one symbolic object or a stronger light axis.
5. Is it too clean? Add grain, paper texture, blur, halation.
6. Is it too tied to one character type? Change the subject to object, room, empty landscape, spirit, city, or symbol.
