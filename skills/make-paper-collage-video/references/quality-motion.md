# Asset, Composite, Motion, and Delivery Quality

Review two scopes:

1. **Assets** — subject completeness, edge integrity, silhouette fidelity, negative space, and background leakage.
2. **Composites** — support contact, inside/on readability, front occlusion, shared carrier motion, registration alignment, boundary integrity, and identity continuity.

A clean hard alpha edge does not prove that a limb or internal hole survived extraction.

The scene camera, group transform, child-local transform, idle motion, and cue transform compose in that order. Groups carry attached families once. Children own only local motion.

Map every approved beat to one cue. Bind critical events to proof moments. Re-render proof evidence after any asset, mask, anchor, boundary, keyframe, cue, or proof-time change.
