# Semantic Production Contracts

A prompt requests an output; a semantic contract defines what must remain true and what evidence must prove it.

## Risk classes

- `decorative`: texture and non-semantic ornament; no contract.
- `identity-critical`: recurring people, animals, or named objects.
- `topology-critical`: limbs, holes, ropes, spokes, and internal negative space.
- `mechanism-critical`: tools, machines, load paths, and functional assemblies.
- `diagram-critical`: text, arrows, labels, and explanatory geometry.

Do not downgrade a critical subject because it is baked into a full-frame plate.

Critical contracts declare invariants and evidence targets. Topology targets require silhouette fidelity, clean negative space, and no background leakage. Mechanism targets declare parts, connections, degrees of freedom, load paths, forbidden forms, and reference evidence. Diagram targets protect semantic lines from procedural distortion.

For coupled composition families, create one complete master and derive all registered members from that same source. Preserve canvas, origin, registration id, and source-master id.
