# Story and Production Planning

Treat duration and scene count as independent constraints. Preserve values explicitly supplied by the human and infer only missing values.

## Profiles

- `draft`: cheapest iteration; reuse complete masters; depth only where essential.
- `balanced`: default; shared character families plus a few independent depth layers.
- `full-depth`: maximum parallax, pose variety, and proof burden.

## Storyboard contract

Every scene needs one narrative role, at least three ordered beats, one bounded blueprint, a composition pattern plan, and three proof moments: establish, action/peak, and final. The final proof stays at or after `0.82` normalized time and outside fades.

Use `supported-subject` for persistent contact, `registered-environment` for a shared semantic boundary, and `free` only for independent elements.
