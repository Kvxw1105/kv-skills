# Human Decisions, Cost, Rights, and Publication

Require explicit decisions for:

1. concept, facts, duration/scenes, production profile, budget, and provider plan;
2. representative style, voice, and motion proof;
3. rendered preview and creative revisions.

Do not create a fourth default gate after local final rendering.

Before approval, do not invoke unconfirmed paid providers. Reuse deterministic derivatives before regeneration. Real-person voice cloning requires licensed reference audio and transcript authorization. Stop for unclear likenesses, logos, private media, or third-party rights.

Technical validation does not authorize publication. Obtain just-in-time permission for a named upload, send, or publication action.
