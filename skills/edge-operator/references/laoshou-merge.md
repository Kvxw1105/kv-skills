## Adopt only behavior-changing mechanisms

1. **Distribution incentive orientation**. For crowded promotional claims ask who commissioned or profits from distribution, but explicitly check paid promotion vs organic imitation vs recommender amplification. Outcome: fewer claims that repetition proves real customers or paid shills.
2. **Shovel-selling triangle**. Record platform/tool owner, promoter/distributor, end customer, each cash flow and refund/fulfillment burden. Outcome: separates advertised riches from who actually receives money. No unverified commission percentages.
3. **Success/recommendation freshness audit**. A streak or repeatedly reused recommendation triggers check of the advantage's remaining conditions, explicit `checked_at` and `invalidate_if`. Outcome: outdated strategies are revisited; streak alone does not prescribe position reduction.

## Reject duplicate scaffolding

Do not import Laoshou's five-tier cash taxonomy over Edge's more precise receipt levels; do not replace the code-backed freeze/settlement ledger with a handwritten ledger; do not replicate twenty near-synonymous thinking atoms. Do not claim all same-script promoters are 'shills' or that an unlucky consenting buyer was necessarily cheated.
