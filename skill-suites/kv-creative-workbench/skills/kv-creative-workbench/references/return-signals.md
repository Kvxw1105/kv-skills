# Return signals v1.0

Use these exact signal names so specialist outputs compose reliably.

## `REFRAME_ROOT`

The original problem definition is producing the wrong solution space.

Owner: language ideation.
Preserve: user evidence, resources, constraints.
Invalidate: framing, candidates, downstream ranking and validation.

## `EXPAND_DIVERSITY`

Candidates vary mainly in labels, features, or tone rather than mechanisms.

Owner: language ideation.
Preserve: strongest frames and any unique candidate core.
Invalidate: weak or duplicate candidates and convergence ranking.

## `REFRAME_TARGET_USER`

The mechanism may work, but the selected user has weak need, access, or fit.

Owner: language ideation, followed by convergence.
Preserve: mechanism and useful interaction pattern.
Invalidate: user fit, scenario, ranking, payer, acquisition, and validation.

## `SPLIT_USER_PAYER`

The beneficiary needs the result but another actor controls payment or adoption.

Owner: business strategist.
Preserve: problem and mechanism.
Invalidate: payer logic, pricing, channel, sales motion, and experiment.

## `PRESERVE_CORE_CHANGE_FORM`

The creative core is valuable but the current product or delivery form is too costly, broad, or fragile.

Owner: convergence.
Preserve: named creative core and target outcome.
Invalidate: product form, scope, feasibility, MVP, and validation.

## `REDUCE_SCOPE`

The concept is valid but cannot be tested within current resources or horizon.

Owner: convergence, then business strategist.
Preserve: problem, user, mechanism.
Invalidate: feature set, operating model, MVP, and test plan.

## `REVALIDATE_DEMAND`

The business case depends on an unsupported pain, urgency, or switching assumption.

Owner: business strategist.
Preserve: candidate as hypothesis.
Invalidate: willingness to pay, forecast, priority, and recommendation confidence.

## `REDESIGN_DISTRIBUTION`

The offer may be useful, but there is no credible path to the first users.

Owner: business strategist.
Preserve: demand and product core.
Invalidate: acquisition path, pricing assumptions tied to that path, and experiment.

## `USER_GOAL_NONCOMMERCIAL`

Commercial analysis is distorting the user's stated objective.

Owner: workbench.
Preserve: creative and use-value analysis.
Invalidate: payer, pricing, revenue, and market-priority fields.

## `STOP_NO_OPPORTUNITY`

A decisive fatal flaw remains after reasonable transformation, or the evidence does not justify continued effort.

Owner: workbench.
Preserve: lessons and rejected assumptions.
Invalidate: no rerun by default.

## `READY_FOR_DECISION`

The candidate has sufficient framing, fit, evidence, tradeoffs, and next-step clarity for a decision.

Owner: workbench.
