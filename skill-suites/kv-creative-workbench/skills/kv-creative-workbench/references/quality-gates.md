# Quality gates

## Gate 0: Objective integrity

- The deliverable and success definition match the user's real goal.
- Commercial framing is used only when relevant.
- Explicit exclusions and constraints are preserved.

## Gate 1: Frame value

- Reframing changes the problem boundary, actor, goal, mechanism, or evaluation rule.
- It is more than synonym replacement.
- At least one reframed question exposes a previously hidden possibility.

## Gate 2: Candidate diversity

- Candidates differ in mechanism, user behavior, medium, value exchange, or operating model.
- Each candidate explains how value is created.
- Novelty does not rely only on names or metaphors.

## Gate 3: Independent convergence

- Generator hype and rankings were removed before review.
- At least one fatal flaw was actively tested.
- Valuable cores were transformed before rejection when feasible.
- The final portfolio does not consist only of safe, ordinary options.

## Gate 4: Opportunity validity

For commercial work, each leading candidate identifies:

- user, payer, and beneficiary;
- pain or job and current substitute;
- why switching could occur now;
- path to first users;
- smallest valid test;
- success threshold and kill criteria;
- evidence gaps.

## Gate 5: Decision quality

- A leading option is recommended when evidence supports one.
- The decisive tradeoff is explicit.
- Confidence matches evidence.
- The next action is concrete, bounded, and capable of changing the decision.

## Gate 6: Delivery honesty

- Current facts are verified when they materially affect the answer.
- Hypotheses are labeled.
- No unexecuted specialist, test, or artifact is presented as completed.
