# Blind review protocol

## Normalization

For each candidate:

1. Hide its original rank.
2. Delete praise and emotional language.
3. Temporarily replace the name with its ID if the name is persuasive.
4. Rewrite the mechanism in one neutral sentence.
5. Separate facts, user-provided evidence, and hypotheses.
6. Estimate resource and dependency burden from explicit information.

## Anti-halo questions

- Would this still seem strong with a boring name?
- Is the mechanism new, or only the presentation?
- Does the candidate solve a different problem, or use a different interface?
- Is popularity being inferred from enthusiasm?
- Is feasibility being inferred from technical possibility?

## Independence rule

Do not inherit the ideation skill's "most imaginative" or "most promising" labels. Reconstruct the judgment from the normalized packet.
