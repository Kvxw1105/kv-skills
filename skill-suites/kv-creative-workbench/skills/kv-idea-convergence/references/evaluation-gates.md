# Evaluation gates

Use gates, not a mechanical average score.

## Gate A: Objective fit

Does the candidate advance the user's actual goal and success definition?

## Gate B: Mechanism integrity

Can the causal mechanism be stated clearly? Does it differ structurally from alternatives?

## Gate C: Reality fit

Can the user or team reach a meaningful test within the stated horizon and resources?

## Gate D: Value intensity

Would the actor use, adopt, share, save, or pay for the result strongly enough for the stated goal?

## Gate E: Validation leverage

Can a small test produce decision-changing evidence without building the full system?

## Gate F: Asset potential

Can the work become a reusable method, dataset, content series, template, workflow, skill, tool, or product line?

## Gate G: Fatal risk

Does the candidate require unacceptable legal, ethical, privacy, platform, data, capital, or distribution exposure?

## Gate logic

- Failure at A or B normally rejects the candidate.
- Failure at C may trigger transformation or scope reduction.
- Failure at D may trigger target-user repair or rejection.
- Failure at E lowers priority unless the strategic value is exceptional.
- Strength at F can justify Archive even when immediate feasibility is weak.
- Failure at G rejects or requires a different form.
