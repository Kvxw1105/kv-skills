# Rejection rules

## Reject without rescue when

- the mechanism is illegal, deceptive, coercive, or privacy-invasive by design;
- the concept depends on a resource the user cannot obtain and that resource is the core;
- the stated need is contradicted by strong evidence and no alternative actor exists;
- the concept has no mechanism after names and aesthetics are removed;
- it conflicts with the user's explicit objective or risk boundary.

## Attempt one rescue when

- the core is original but the form is too large;
- the user is wrong but a nearby payer or beneficiary exists;
- validation is expensive but a manual or content proxy is possible;
- product scope is broad but one high-pain moment can be isolated;
- the current channel is weak but demand may still be real.

## Archive when

- timing is wrong but the core is durable;
- required assets can be accumulated later;
- the concept is useful as a method, content series, or research direction;
- it should not consume current execution capacity.

## Do not use false balance

If one option is materially stronger, recommend it. Preserve alternatives only when they serve distinct portfolio roles or depend on unresolved evidence.
