# KV Idea Convergence

An independent review skill that filters, rescues, transforms, and prioritizes ideas while preserving valuable creative cores.

## Who should use it

Use it after brainstorming or whenever a user has many candidate directions and needs a small, defensible portfolio.

## Trigger examples

- Blind-review these ten ideas and keep only the strongest three.
- This concept is imaginative but too large. Preserve its core and redesign the form.
- Classify these directions as promote, transform, archive, or reject.

Near miss: "Give me ten fresh names for this project." That belongs to ideation.

## Workflow

The skill removes generator hype, applies gate-based review, extracts creative cores, transforms weak forms, and sends at most three finalists downstream.

## Requirements and limitations

No external tool is required. Quality depends on having a clear goal and constraints, but reasonable defaults are used when missing. It can operate alone or inside IdeaCase v1.0.

## Installation

Upload the packaged `skill.zip` through the ChatGPT Skills interface.

## Privacy

The package contains no credentials, private data, scripts, or external assets.
