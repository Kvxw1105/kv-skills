# KV Business Opportunity Strategist

A commercial validation skill that turns an idea into a falsifiable thesis with actors, demand, distribution, MVP experiments, and kill criteria.

## Who should use it

Use it for product viability, monetization, payer logic, market entry, first-user acquisition, MVP design, or deciding whether an opportunity deserves more investment.

## Trigger examples

- Evaluate whether this agent idea is worth building and design a paid pilot.
- Separate the user and payer, then test willingness to pay.
- The need seems real but I have no channel. Redesign the first-user path.

Near miss: "Develop the lore of my fictional universe without discussing monetization." Commercial validation should not take over that task.

## Workflow

The skill maps market actors, defines the demand and wedge thesis, chooses the lowest-complexity offer, designs distribution and validation, and sets explicit kill criteria.

## Requirements and limitations

Current market, competitor, pricing, regulation, and platform claims require live authoritative research in the host environment. The package does not contain market data and does not fabricate it.

## Installation

Upload the packaged `skill.zip` through the ChatGPT Skills interface.

## Privacy

The package contains no credentials or private data. Users should avoid providing unnecessary sensitive customer information during validation work.
