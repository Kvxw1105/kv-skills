# Kill criteria

Define criteria before the test whenever possible.

## Demand kill

Stop or change the actor when relevant users do not report recent behavior, cost, urgency, or commitment after a sufficient targeted sample.

## Payment kill

Stop the current offer when the identified payer repeatedly values the result below delivery cost or refuses any meaningful commitment despite verified pain.

## Distribution kill

Change the channel when the first-user path requires inaccessible trust, permissions, or acquisition cost and no adjacent channel is credible.

## Delivery kill

Reduce scope or stop when reliable delivery requires unavailable data, excessive human review, unacceptable liability, or integrations that cannot be tested incrementally.

## Retention kill

Change the use pattern when value is episodic and the model assumes frequent use. Consider event-triggered or one-off pricing instead of forcing subscription.

## Founder or operator fit kill

Archive when the opportunity requires capabilities, relationships, or operating work the user explicitly will not build, unless a partner path is realistic.

## Ethics and compliance kill

Stop any design whose value depends on deception, coercion, privacy invasion, unlawful access, discriminatory exclusion, or unmanageable professional risk.

## Criteria quality

A kill criterion must include a condition, threshold, time box, and next decision. Avoid vague statements such as "if users do not like it."
