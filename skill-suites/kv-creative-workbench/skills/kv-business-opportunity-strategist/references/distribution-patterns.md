# Distribution patterns

Choose a first-user motion that matches trust, urgency, and product form.

## Existing audience

Use owned content, community, mailing list, clients, or partners. Strong when the user already has trust and a repeated problem narrative.

## Problem-location entry

Enter where the triggering work already happens: professional groups, forums, search queries, workflow tools, events, procurement moments, or service providers.

## Service-led entry

Deliver manually to a narrow segment, learn the workflow, then standardize. Strong for complex or high-trust problems.

## Content-led entry

Publish diagnostics, benchmarks, cases, tools, or public analyses that demonstrate judgment. Strong when education and trust precede purchase.

## Partner-led entry

Work through experts, agencies, schools, firms, associations, or software providers that already control access.

## Workflow integration

Embed into an existing tool or process. Strong when switching costs are high and the new value can appear inside current behavior.

## Trigger-based outreach

Reach actors when a public or observable event creates urgency: new role, filing deadline, product launch, policy change, exam, funding, migration, or incident.

## Distribution check

- Can the first 20 relevant users be named or located?
- Is the message tied to a triggering moment?
- Why should they trust the offer?
- What action is requested?
- What result arrives before substantial commitment?
- What platform or intermediary can remove access?
