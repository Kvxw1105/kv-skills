# Validation experiments

Test the most dangerous assumption, not the easiest metric.

## Problem interview with behavior evidence

Ask about the last occurrence, current workaround, time or money spent, failed attempts, and consequences. Do not rely on hypothetical interest.

## Manual delivery

Perform the proposed outcome manually for a small number of users. Measure completion, repeat demand, willingness to provide inputs, and payment.

## Paid pilot

Offer a bounded result to a defined segment. Payment, signed commitment, or budget approval is stronger evidence than praise.

## Concierge prototype

Use forms, spreadsheets, Notion, prompts, and human judgment behind a simple interface. Test workflow before automation.

## Landing-page or message test

Use only when traffic is relevant and the requested action is meaningful. A click is weak; booked call, deposit, submitted data, or completed qualification is stronger.

## Content proxy

Publish a recurring analysis or diagnostic and observe saves, qualified inbound, repeat readership, data submissions, or requests for execution.

## Switching test

Ask users to replace one part of their current process for a time box. Measure whether they complete the switch and return.
