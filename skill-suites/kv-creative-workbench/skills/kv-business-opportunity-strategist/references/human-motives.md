# Human motives

Select only motives that causally affect adoption.

## Economic

- earn revenue;
- reduce labor or cost;
- prevent loss;
- increase throughput;
- improve decision quality.

## Control and certainty

- reduce uncertainty;
- avoid mistakes;
- make progress visible;
- gain auditability;
- retain ownership or privacy.

## Effort and convenience

- avoid repetitive work;
- avoid learning a complex process;
- reduce coordination;
- eliminate switching between tools;
- get a result faster.

## Identity and status

- appear competent;
- gain professional credibility;
- join a valued group;
- create social proof;
- protect reputation.

## Emotional and social

- reduce anxiety or embarrassment;
- feel understood;
- gain accountability;
- receive confirmation;
- create a shareable story.

## Motive test

For every selected motive, answer:

- What behavior reveals it?
- Is it strong enough to change action?
- Does the payer share the motive?
- What substitute currently satisfies it?
