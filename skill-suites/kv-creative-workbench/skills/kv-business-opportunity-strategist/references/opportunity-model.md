# Opportunity model

Evaluate an opportunity as a chain. A weak decisive link can invalidate the whole thesis.

## 1. Problem intensity

- frequency;
- severity;
- urgency;
- financial, time, emotional, compliance, or status cost;
- consequence of delay.

## 2. Existing behavior

- current workaround;
- existing spend;
- repeated manual labor;
- expert or employee time;
- avoidance, delay, or error;
- communities where the problem is discussed.

## 3. Switching force

- a new regulation or platform shift;
- rising cost;
- new technical capability;
- moment of transition;
- visible failure of the current method;
- trust or distribution advantage.

## 4. Wedge sharpness

- narrow actor;
- triggering moment;
- single high-value outcome;
- limited workflow;
- clear promise;
- reachable first users.

## 5. Delivery feasibility

- required data and access;
- reliability standard;
- human review;
- integration burden;
- support burden;
- legal and privacy exposure.

## 6. Distribution feasibility

- source of trust;
- channel access;
- message clarity;
- sales or onboarding friction;
- repeatable acquisition potential.

## 7. Economic or strategic value

- willingness to pay or budget owner;
- cost to deliver;
- frequency and retention;
- expansion path;
- data, workflow, audience, or authority asset created.

Do not reduce the model to one average score. Identify the bottleneck and the evidence needed to resolve it.
