# Breakthrough mode

## Strong transforms

### Metaphor as system

Choose a distant system and map:

- actors;
- resources;
- rules;
- feedback;
- rewards;
- failure;
- time pattern.

Generate a concept only after the mapping changes at least three design variables.

### Extreme constraint

Remove a default resource: screen, text, money, attention, expert, platform, memory, or user effort. Then design around the absence.

### Opposite objective

Reverse the stated goal and search for value in slowness, forgetting, uncertainty, public failure, or deliberate incompleteness.

### Hidden market

Treat repeated workaround behavior as a market signal. Ask what users already assemble manually and what identity or risk makes them hide the need.

### Temporal displacement

Design the concept for ten years earlier, ten years later, one day only, or a lifetime. Extract the mechanism that survives time.

## Guardrails

- Do not confuse taboo language with insight.
- Do not rely on illegal, deceptive, coercive, or privacy-invasive behavior.
- Do not let a fictional world replace the user's actual problem unless fiction is the goal.
- Do not use mystical claims as causal explanations.
