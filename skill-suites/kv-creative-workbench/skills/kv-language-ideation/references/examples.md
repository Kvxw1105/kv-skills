# Examples

## Example: "AI writing tool"

Weak divergence:

- AI editor;
- AI outline generator;
- AI title generator.

These share the same mechanism: generate or edit text on request.

Stronger frame shifts:

- Writing as evidence collection.
- Writing as rehearsal for an argument.
- Writing as a long-term model of the author's judgment.
- Writing as coordination between author, reader, and future self.

Candidate examples:

- `I-01 Argument Flight Simulator`: stress-tests claims against modeled opponents before drafting.
- `I-02 Evidence Ledger`: turns notes, sources, and claims into a traceable proof structure.
- `I-03 Voice Memory`: learns recurring judgment patterns and flags when a draft contradicts them.

The names matter less than the distinct mechanisms: adversarial rehearsal, evidence traceability, and longitudinal consistency.
