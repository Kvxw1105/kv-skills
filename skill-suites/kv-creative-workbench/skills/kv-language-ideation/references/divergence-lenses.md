# Divergence lenses

Use a mix of distant lenses. Do not generate all candidates from one cluster.

## Mechanism lenses

- diagnosis and feedback;
- ritual and commitment;
- simulation and rehearsal;
- matching and coordination;
- marketplace and exchange;
- automation and delegation;
- curation and reduction;
- game rules and progression;
- social proof and public identity;
- memory and longitudinal context;
- constraint and deliberate friction;
- environment or infrastructure.

## Value lenses

- remove labor;
- reduce uncertainty;
- improve judgment;
- create identity or status;
- prevent loss;
- increase control;
- generate evidence;
- coordinate people;
- make an invisible process visible;
- turn one-off work into a reusable asset.

## Diversity test

For each pair of candidates, ask whether a user could switch between them without changing behavior. If yes, they may be feature variants rather than different concepts.

A valid set should vary across at least three of these axes:

- actor;
- mechanism;
- medium;
- value unit;
- business or exchange model;
- time pattern;
- social structure.
